## Short description (≤ 140 chars)
Pairwise: market-neutral pairs-trading vaults for tokenized stocks. Stake $PAIR to share protocol fees and propose new pairs.

## One-liner
Long one stock, short its twin, earn the spread — on-chain.

## Longer description (≤ 500 chars)
$PAIR is the token of Pairwise, a protocol of market-neutral vaults that trade tokenized stocks in pairs: when two
correlated stocks drift apart, a vault buys the cheap one, shorts the rich one in equal dollars, and exits when they
converge. Every decision is computed on-chain from oracle prices. $PAIR stakers earn a share of the vaults' performance
fees and can propose new pairs for listing. Experimental software; trading can lose money.
